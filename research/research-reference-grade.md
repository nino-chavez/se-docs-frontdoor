## Framework translation used (stated so every grade below is reproducible)

This initiative makes no visual-design recommendations; it makes architecture and tooling recommendations. The two-track framework is translated once, here, rather than case-by-case — an unstated translation is precisely how the prior run admitted a vendor advocacy post into the Quality track.

**Quality track, for an architecture/tooling memo, requires one of:**
- Vendor-primary documentation for a **capability claim about that vendor's own product** (the analogue of "design-system documentation authored by the reference owner").
- A standards-body publication **with the specific entry cited**.
- A primary defect report **for a defect on the named surface**.
- Measured research for an **empirical** claim (adoption rates, retention, incidence).

**Convention track** covers market presence, category dominance, pricing position, name recognition — and, load-bearing here: **vendor or competitor advocacy for an architecture choice is marketing, not quality evidence.** That discriminator is what separates "the Citations API exists and no official remote MCP server ships for Drive" (a capability fact about Anthropic's own product, on which Anthropic is authoritative) from "federated search beats indexed RAG" (an architecture verdict, on which an authorization vendor is not neutral).

## What this run could and could not re-derive

This gate ran without web-fetch or shell capability. **No external URL in the corpus was fetched.** That constraint is reported rather than concealed, and it does not carry the verdict: every blocking finding below is established from the corpus alone, in one of three shapes —

1. **No citation exists at all** — adoption statistics, the OWASP #2 ranking, the EchoLeak incident, Glean's ~$60k/yr floor.
2. **A citation exists but cannot support the claim's *type*** — Oso, a vendor, for an architecture verdict.
3. **An evidence column names sources absent from the corpus** — "practitioner write-ups"; "Guru / Notion AI / Atlassian Rovo … surveyed in sources doc".

None of those required a fetch. Conversely, references whose evidence is a specific URL to the vendor's own reference page **pass Check 3** — that check tests evidence presence and shape, not evidence truth. The unfetchability is applied instead to the corpus's *"verified" / "confirmed" labels*, which are downgraded below to "cited, not re-derived in this gate run."

## Findings

1. **`REF_NO_EVIDENCE` — the adoption statistics have no source.** `research/sources/definition-and-grill-2026-07-09.md:21`. "Median enterprise doc-bot < 15% weekly-active by month 6", "citations are the #1 adoption driver", and "standard mitigations" are three empirical claims with zero citation — the only market-scan bullet without a URL, in a doc that attests on line 3 that every load-bearing claim carries one. They propagate to `research/problem-space/problem-statement.md:104` (canonical), `research/funnel/pilot-funnel.md:38`, and `docs/se-team-brief.html:252`. *Remediation:* supply a measured source (a named survey/study with author, outlet, year, n) or strike the numbers. **`problem-statement.md:104` is one sentence** — it can drop the appeal to the statistic and stand on P5/P6 plus the kickoff 00:24:47 evidence, which is where the invariant's real force already comes from.
2. **`QUALITY_CLAIM_CONVENTION_GROUNDED` — the retrieval "consensus" rests on a vendor arguing for its own product category.** `definition-and-grill:20`, propagated to `buy-landscape.md:20–21`, `workspace-prior-art.md:26`, `se-team-brief.html:258`. Oso sells authorization-as-a-service; a post titled "the right approach to authorization in RAG" is category advocacy, and one such post is not a consensus. *Remediation:* re-ground in sources authoritative for the claim type — Anthropic's own retrieval guidance (already cited in this corpus for the 200K threshold), the OWASP entry cited properly, or a measured incident writeup — **or** restate the claim at the strength the evidence supports: *"the federated-over-indexed argument is advanced by authorization vendors; our own uniform-ACL + live-source inventory is the independent reason it fits here."* That second reason is already in the corpus and does not need Oso.
3. **`REF_NO_EVIDENCE` — the OWASP #2 ranking is uncited and internally inconsistent.** No URL, version, or entry ID appears anywhere. `definition-and-grill:20` says "ACL-sync drift leak is #2"; both grading tables say "Sensitive Information Disclosure ranked #2". Those are different claims about the same rank. *Remediation:* cite the OWASP publication with its version and entry ID, and pick one statement of what is ranked.
4. **`REF_NO_EVIDENCE` — "EchoLeak" and "vector-DB bypass" are named as real incidents with no citation.** `definition-and-grill:20`. *Remediation:* cite or strike.
5. **`REF_NO_EVIDENCE` — Glean's ~$60k/yr floor is uncited and cannot come from the source claimed.** `definition-and-grill:19`, `buy-landscape.md:13`, `se-team-brief.html:250`. The corpus calls the vendor sales-led — i.e. no public price list — so "vendor-published Slack-container docs" cannot be the price's source. This number retires an option. *Remediation:* name where the figure came from (analyst note, quote received, third-party report with date) or mark it an estimate and label it as such wherever it appears.
6. **`REF_UNCLASSIFIED` — two phantom citations in the grading tables.** (a) "Practitioner write-ups on federated vs indexed retrieval" — plural, named in both evidence columns, absent from the corpus. (b) "Guru / Notion AI / Atlassian Rovo … surveyed in sources doc" — these three appear in no research file except the grading tables that claim they were surveyed. *Remediation:* add the write-ups and the survey, or delete the rows. A grading table that cites its own missing homework is worse than an incomplete one. Note that "re-classify" is the wrong remedy for these two: there is nothing to classify until a source exists.
7. **Prior-gate method failure — top priority, because it generalizes.** The 2026-07-09 run PASSED. It did not fail on the items it checked; its **method** cannot reach these findings. Reference extraction walks *named* references, so an unsourced claim — which names none — is structurally invisible to it, and the corpus's single most load-bearing empirical claim never entered the graded table. Its Check 4 phrase list is literal, so the synonyms doing the actual quality work evaded it: "consensus" (line 20), "canon" (line 22), "standard mitigations" (line 21), "in the data" (`se-team-brief.html:252`), "proven" (`se-team-brief.html:243`). And it accepted the corpus's own self-classification and its own "grep swept" attestation as evidence — the circular audit this gate exists to break. *Remediation for future runs:* extract **claims** first and *then* look for the reference, rather than extracting references and then looking for claims; treat "consensus", "canon(ical)", "standard", "beats", "proven", "in the data", and "#1/#2" ranking assertions as trigger phrases.
8. **Evidence-shape mismatch — NotebookLM.** `research/current-state/source-and-org-landscape.md:14` and `definition-and-grill:39`. A claim about API surface cites `/gemini/enterprise/docs/choose-product`, a product-selection page; no API reference is cited. *Remediation:* cite the Enterprise API reference, and downgrade "(verified)" to "(cited)" until someone re-derives it against that page.
9. **Caveat dropped in transit — `anthropics/claude-code#53442`.** The sources doc correctly distinguishes the Cowork/MCP connector (where the defect is filed) from the claude.ai Enterprise connector (where the risk lives, "undocumented"). `buy-landscape.md:33` and the prior grading table cite the issue for the Enterprise-connector risk without that distinction. *Remediation:* carry the surface-transfer caveat into the evidence column wherever the issue is cited.
10. **Warn `REF_BALANCE_CONVENTION_HEAVY`.** By strict track count, 6 of 15 references are Convention-only — **40%, which does not reach the 50% threshold.** The warn fires on **effective weighting** instead, per the Rally HQ precedent (§19.2, where 50% strict understated a Convention-dominant recommendation): 11 of 15 references (73%) cannot anchor a quality claim, because five of the Quality-asserted ones resolve to no source. The four that survive are all vendor-primary docs about their own products — excellent for capability facts, and none of them speaks to the two claims that actually drove design decisions. This warn does not carry the verdict; findings 1–6 do. *Remediation:* the pool needs at least one measured, non-vendor source for the empirical adoption claim and one for the retrieval-architecture claim.
11. **Warn `QUALITY_SOLE_SOURCE`.** After the Oso downgrade and the OWASP failure, **no** Quality-track reference in this corpus speaks to the retrieval-architecture decision. This is worse than sole-source: the decision's quality grounding is empty, and it is currently held up by the initiative's own situational reasoning (uniform ACLs, live-source inventory, freshness sensitivity). That reasoning is sound and sufficient for *this* initiative — it should be stated as the reason, replacing the borrowed authority.
12. **Warn — pricing and capability claims uncited across the buy shelf.** Onyx $0–20/seat; Dust $30–150/seat (contradicted as "$0–$30/seat" at `se-team-brief.html:250`); Agentforce "2026-03 overhaul" and "6,000+ apps"; Gong "not a standard Claude Enterprise connector" (which fires ADR-0001 trigger 3). Non-blocking individually — the buy lane is retired on other grounds — but the Gong claim gates a build trigger and should carry the connector-catalogue citation.
13. **Warn — "proven" on the org rails.** `docs/se-team-brief.html:243` calls the existing rails "proven by an adjacent internal POC". The claim is first-party and plausible, but it rests on one internal doc that is named nowhere with a path or link. *Remediation:* downgrade to "observed in", and give the GEM POC doc a durable location the way the kickoff PDF was flagged for one.
